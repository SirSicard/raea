Not everyone who needs to see your work uses raea. This tutorial shares a cut on a link, follows a reviewer's notes
back to the agent who made it, and puts the next version on the same link.

## Share a cut

Open the cut and press Share, at the top right. Name the link, choose how long it works, and press Create link. raea
sends the file to your storage, and Copy gives you the link. Reviewers need no account, and take no seat on your plan.

## What your reviewer sees

The link opens a player on raea.io. The reviewer gives their name, clicks the picture where something should change,
and writes the note right there, at that frame. They can step through frame by frame, and Approve marks the version
approved.

## Their notes come back

Every note they leave comes back to the item in raea, pinned where they put it, with their name on it. Send it to the
agent like one of your own. Reply, or mark it done, and the reviewer sees that on the page.

## The next version, on the same link

When the agent's next version lands, open it, and the Share panel says the link still shows the earlier one. Press
Make it v2. The same link now plays v2, with a picker for every version, and Compare plays two side by side.

## Every link in one place

Library, in the sidebar, has Review links: every link you've sent, as a card saying where it stands. Approved, notes to
address, waiting, or ended, with its views and comments. Stop sharing ends a link for everyone, at once.

## Several files on one link

In a cloud library, pick the files to send with the box at a tile's corner, or Command-click. Press Share for review in
the bar, and give the link a name. Your reviewer gets every file as a poster, and each opens its own page.

## Let your agent share

An agent can share for you too, once you turn on Let agents share in Settings, under Sharing. Ask it to send the cut to
your client: it hands you the link, and it reads the notes as they come in.

## Who can share

Review links come with Pro and Team, because the file is sent from your cloud storage. Downloads stay off unless you
allow them, a password is up to you, and a link is never listed or found by search.
