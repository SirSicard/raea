Reviewing is most of what you do in raea. This tutorial opens a cut, pins a note on the frame it's about, sends it to
the agent who made it, and follows the answer back into the feed.

## Open an item

Click a card in the feed to open it. Each kind of work opens in a view made for it. A cut opens in a player. A still
opens large, to look at. A sound opens with its waveform. And a game opens live, so you can play it.

## Watch it through

Press play and watch the cut through once. Beside the picture are the notes already left on it, each one with the
moment it's about, so you can see what's been asked before you add to it.

## Pin a note

When something should change, choose Comment, and click the place in the frame your note is about. The pin holds that
place and that moment. Write the note in plain words, the way you'd say it to the person making it. A note always knows
what it's about, so the agent gets the frame, the place, and your words together.

## Send it to an agent

Post the note, then send it to the agent who made the piece. The project's Agents panel shows that it's working. Go
back to the feed and keep reviewing while it does.

## The next version

When the agent is done, its answer lands in the feed as the next version of the same item, not as a new one. Earlier
versions stay with it, so nothing is lost, and the notes it answered are marked as answered.

## Status

Every item moves through the same five steps: planned, making, posted, seen, and done. Opening an item marks it seen,
and its status shows beside its title. The number beside the Inbox counts what's posted and not seen yet, so you always
know what's waiting for you.
